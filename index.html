Milestone Progression

The 4-Stage Swimmer's Journey

A transparent, step-by-step roadmap from your first hesitant splash to effortless competitive speed.

01 0 - 30 Days

Water Comfortness

Eliminate water anxiety, master relaxed breathing rhythm, and achieve natural starfish floating in water.

  • Rhythmic Bobbing (20x)
  • 60s Relaxed Starfish Float
  • 15m Streamline Flutter Kick
Target: Fear-Free Swimmer
02 1 - 3 Months

4-Stroke Mechanics

Learn high-elbow catch Freestyle, rotational Backstroke, Breaststroke whip kicks, and Dolphin waves.

  • 50m Continuous Freestyle
  • Bilateral 3-Count Breathing
  • 4K Video Stroke Review
Target: 4-Stroke Mastery
03 3 - 6 Months

Endurance & Flip Turns

Build aerobic stamina to swim 1,000+ meters effortlessly, execute seamless wall tumble turns, and sprint intervals.

  • 500m Non-Stop Lap Swim
  • High-Speed Tumble Turns
  • Dive Starts from Blocks
Target: 1,000m+ Lap Endurance
04 6+ Months

Podium Dominance

Full preparation for SFI state meets, national qualifiers, or open-water triathlons with race taper strategies.

  • Sub-28s 50m Free Target
  • Race Day Taper Strategy
  • Official SFI Meet License
Target: State / National Podium
