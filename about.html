Our Story

Born from a Passion for Water

AquaElite Swimming Academy was founded in 2009 by former national swimmer Coach Rajesh Kumar. His vision was simple: to create a world-class aquatic training centre where every swimmer — from fearful beginners to ambitious competitors — could achieve their fullest potential.

Starting with a single pool and three coaches, we've grown into one of India's premier aquatic academies, with over 3,500 students trained, multiple national champions produced, and a facility that rivals international standards.
